01 — Featured Project

Wave3 Collective

A production e-commerce platform and fully custom CMS for a Philippine streetwear brand — storefront, real-time inventory, payment verification, and bookkeeping. The owner runs the entire business through it without touching code.

Live Site ↗ Full Case Study GitHub

What it does

  • Custom CMS — products, pricing, content, and money reporting managed by a non-technical owner
  • Race-condition-safe inventory — stock reserved in DB transactions; two buyers can't get the last unit
  • Shopee-style payment flow — reserve → upload proof → verify → approve, with auto-expiry and restock
  • Self-healing bookkeeping — every sale auto-logs income and shipping expenses; CSV/Excel exports
  • Multi-currency storefront — PHP / USD / USDT with live FX conversion

Result & stack

  • V1 sold out its first production drop — real customers, real payments, real fulfillment
  • Zero developer dependency — owner operates the whole business day to day through the CMS
  • Launched months earlier — manual payment verification put the business in market before gateway onboarding
